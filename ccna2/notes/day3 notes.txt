COMPRESS IPv6 addresses
1. a:c::/64
2. ::/0
3. fe80::a:0:0:f/64 = Link-local
4. 2002:6500:0:3000::/64
5. ::1/128 = Loopback/local host
6. ff00::beef:a00a:aa0:0:0/8 = multicast address
